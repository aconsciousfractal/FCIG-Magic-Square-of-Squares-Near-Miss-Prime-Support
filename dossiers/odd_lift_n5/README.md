# Odd-lift frontier

## Origin

The reconstruction tower of Section 9 stratifies an outer-primitive branch
by an odd vertical coefficient. Its symmetry produces Kummer, elliptic and
higher-genus quotients.

## Closed first lift

For coefficient `n = 3`, certified-complete rational-point lists on the two
genus-two quotients exclude every nondegenerate lift. This is a closure of
one branch, not a global Parker theorem.

## Next lift

For `n = 5`, the live branch reduces over `Q(sqrt(2))` to two genus-two
covers. One is empty on the required real component. The other has a
complete fake two-Selmer set of four classes, none eliminated. A rank-two
pair-field lattice is saturated at the tested primes, but finite-index
Mordell-Weil control over the associated degree-30 field is not proved.

## Open boundary

There is no complete rational-point list for the surviving `n = 5` cover
and no uniform theorem for larger odd coefficients. Recorded automatic-CAS
timeouts are computational limits, not mathematical evidence of emptiness.
