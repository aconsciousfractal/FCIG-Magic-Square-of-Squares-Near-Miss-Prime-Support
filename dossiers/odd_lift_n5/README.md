# Odd-lift frontier

## Origin and evidence scope

The reconstruction tower summarized in Appendix C stratifies an
outer-primitive branch by an odd vertical coefficient. Its symmetry gives
Kummer, elliptic and higher-genus quotients. This dossier records the
continuation's state; the public main-paper verifier does not reproduce
its higher-lift calculations.

## The coefficient-3 specialization result

For `n=3`, the recorded complete rational-point lists on two genus-two
quotients exclude every nondegenerate rational specialization of the known
generic coefficient-3 family that satisfies both base reconstruction
conditions. This result does not classify all specialized Mordell–Weil
points outside the generic subgroup and does not close every possible
coefficient-3 construction.

## The coefficient-5 continuation

For `n=5`, the relevant branch reduces over `Q(sqrt(2))` to two genus-two
covers. One is empty on the required real component. The surviving cover
has a complete fake two-Selmer set of four classes, none eliminated.

The known pair generates a lattice `M=<H,R>` isomorphic to `Z^2`.
It proves a rank lower bound of two and is saturated at `2,3,5,7`.
There is no proved rank upper bound, finite-index statement for `M` in
the full Mordell–Weil group over the associated degree-30 field, or full
saturation result.

## Open boundary

There is no complete list of points on the surviving cover with the
required rational coordinate `T`, and no uniform theorem for larger odd
coefficients. Recorded automatic-CAS timeouts and unsuccessful bounded
searches are computational limits, not evidence of emptiness.
