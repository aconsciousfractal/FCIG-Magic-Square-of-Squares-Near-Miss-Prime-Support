# Exhausted routes and their limits

This catalogue prevents computational no-hits from being mistaken for
theorems. It is deliberately phrased without references to private logs.

| route | established boundary |
|---|---|
| direct high-degree class-group computation | exceeds the governed memory/time scale; this is cost evidence only |
| short-vector searches | found no witness; a miss cannot prove non-principality |
| elementary `S`-unit candidates | yielded only already-known multiplicative directions |
| descent-image supply | collapses to the marked class modulo the known unit direction, conditional on the recorded Mordell-Weil generators being complete |
| pure-pair compression rows | span an even-weight space and cannot certify the odd marked vector |
| finite Kummer-class obstruction | cannot detect the interaction equation; explicit counterexamples occur in every depth |
| automatic genus-two descent shortcuts | software errors or timeouts were replaced by explicit local checks where a theorem is stated |
| bounded census and selector searches | support only their displayed finite bounds and never an unbounded nonexistence claim |

The remaining open problems are stated in the three dossier READMEs. No
route listed here proves or disproves the existence of a `3 x 3` magic
square of nine distinct rational squares.
