# Open-problem dossiers

Section 9 of the paper separates proved results from three unresolved
arithmetic directions. These public dossiers are self-contained summaries;
their statements and evidence require only the files shipped here.

| dossier | exact public boundary |
|---|---|
| [`residual_cover/`](residual_cover/README.md) | one residual covering class over `Q(sqrt(218))`; neither a rational point nor emptiness is proved |
| [`odd_lift_n5/`](odd_lift_n5/README.md) | the coefficient-3 lift is closed; for coefficient 5, one of two genus-two covers over `Q(sqrt(2))` is empty on the required real component and the other retains four fake two-Selmer classes without a complete rational-point list |
| [`degree90_sclass/`](degree90_sclass/README.md) | a marked class in a degree-90 `S`-class group remains undecided |

[`EXHAUSTED_ROUTES.md`](EXHAUSTED_ROUTES.md) records methods that were tried
and the precise limits of the resulting evidence. None of these dossiers
adds a theorem to the paper or claims a solution of the Parker problem.
