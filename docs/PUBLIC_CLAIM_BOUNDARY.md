# Public claim boundary

- **The object is the difference side**: the common difference `2d` of the
  three square progressions forced by the completion problem, and its
  prime support. The entry-side lineage (which primes divide which
  *entries* of a completed square — Rabern, Woll, Labruna, Weisenberg) is
  attributed prior art; nothing here strengthens, re-proves, or competes
  with it, and Theorem 6–7 depend on none of it.
- **No four-prime exclusion is claimed.** Section 8 states the frontier
  exactly; the six addition-curve fibres, the outer fixed cores beyond
  area 6, and the unbalanced four-prime cores are open.
- **Nothing here proves or refutes the existence** of a 3x3 magic square
  of nine distinct positive squares. The taxonomy of §9 gives necessary
  minima and matching conditions, not existence results.
- **No effectivity**: the support-law count (imported from
  Evertse–Schlickewei–Schmidt) counts and never enumerates; no effective
  procedure and no global support-size bound follows.
- **The finite-class stop (Thm 5) has exact scope**: it rules out
  obstructions retaining only the three finite Kummer/2-Selmer classes on
  the ambient doubled-point space; representative-level methods are not
  excluded.
- **Censuses are bounded**: the `(8,7)` census is frozen at its stated
  height bound and is never used as evidence for an unbounded statement;
  only the frozen default bound is citable.
- **Commercial-CAS evidence enters only as hash-verified frozen
  transcripts** (with their exact calculator inputs); the verifier never
  requires that software to run.
- **No priority, novelty, or firstness** is asserted for any statement.
  Where the record says no exact antecedent was located, that is a dated
  negative search, not a certificate. The classical inputs are the
  property of their authors and are used only as attributed prior art; in
  particular the equal-area triangle classification layer inside Theorem 7
  is Aebi's, and no priority is asserted for the join.
- **Three claim-use obligations are deliberately open**: the human
  reviews of the C/D lane behind Theorem 2(i)–(ii) — proofs, certificates
  and their dependencies, which include the Appendix-A census claim
  (`PO-081`) — of the elliptic-orbit lift and its certificate lineage
  used from §2 onward (`PO-089`), and of the imported core of Theorem 4
  (`PO-094`), have not been commissioned. The owner's recorded decision
  is to proceed with the cautious claim-use wording those open gates
  require, and the manuscript keeps it everywhere. Machine replays,
  independent-implementation checks and adversarial agent reviews exist
  in the project record but do not substitute for those human gates.
- The dossiers declare **open states** (J458 at `q_R(K)`; the lift tower
  at four uneliminated fake-Selmer classes for `n = 5`; the degree-90
  class `[A_R]`); no result beyond the recorded ones is claimed there.
