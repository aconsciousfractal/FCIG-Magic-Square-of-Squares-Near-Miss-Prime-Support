\section{Conclusions and the computational program}\label{sec:program}

The theorems of this paper close two whole support classes and one fibre,
and they map the frontier exactly. This closing section summarizes the
program that organizes what remains, and states where each open object
lives. Everything here is a summary: the citable record is the companion
repository's dossiers and exhausted-routes catalogue, while the full
statements and their certificates remain in the unpublished project
record behind this paper.

\subsection{The difference-side taxonomy}

For each progression write $U_i=(t_i-r_i)/2$, $V_i=(t_i+r_i)/2$, so
$U_iV_i=d/2$ and $U_i^2+V_i^2=s_i^2$. An odd support prime is interior at
most at one vertex, and its allowed interior vertices depend only on
$p\bmod8$: for $p\equiv1$ all of $\{0,1,2\}$; for $p\equiv3$ none; for
$p\equiv5$ only the centre $\{0\}$; for $p\equiv7$ only the outer vertices
$\{1,2\}$. The number of primitive progressions is
$3-\#\{\text{distinct occupied interior vertices}\}\ge\max(0,5-\omega(2d))$.
The eight labelled incidence strata have exact arithmetic minima for the
common difference ($24$, $600$, $1{,}176$, $29{,}400$, $339{,}864$,
$8{,}496{,}600$ for the deepest stratum), and the zero-primitive stratum
passes its combinatorial gate exactly under the Hall conditions
\[
n_1+n_5\ge1,\qquad n_1+n_7\ge2,\qquad n_1+n_5+n_7\ge3
\]
on the counts of extra primes by residue class. \textbf{These are necessary
minima and matching conditions, not existence results}, and no Diophantine
statement follows from the taxonomy alone. The entry-side counterpart of
this classification --- which primes divide which \emph{entries} --- is the
lineage of Rabern, Woll, Labruna and
Weisenberg~\cite{RAB03,WOL18,LAB18,WEI23} discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:intro}. On the centre side, the catalogue of Gaussian
representations of the square centre parallels the signature-based
organization proposed by Weisenberg~\cite{WEI23}, \S3.

\subsection{The compiler and the outer reduction}

A fail-closed compiler accepts the complete factorization of $2d$ with the
three valuation rows, raises a typed violation of any encoded necessary
law, routes exact supports $\{2,3\}$ and $\{2,3,p\}$ to the exclusions of
Sections~\ref{sec:supportlaw}--\ref{sec:threeprime}, and otherwise emits
one canonical branch key with its successor problem --- an interface
closure, deliberately not a Diophantine one. For the two-primitive-AP outer
branch, the canonical edge factors are pairwise coprime with exactly one
divisible by four; the two normalized odd norms share a unique squarefree
kernel; every odd prime common to them is $1\bmod8$; and the branch admits
an explicit double-cover presentation --- the entry point of the surface
geometry of Section~\ref{sec:surface}.

\subsection{Where the open objects live}

The companion repository carries three dossiers, each declaring its
terminal state exactly:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{J458}: the descent chain growing out of the area-30/60
covering classes of Section~\ref{sec:frontier}, developed through explicit
higher descents to a single residual class $q_R$ over $\Q(\sqrt{218})$;
open at exactly the decision $q_R(K)$ --- a point gives rank two, emptiness
gives rank one with a nontrivial divisible Tate--Shafarevich class.
\item \textbf{The lift tower} ($n\ge3$): the reconstruction covers of the
transversal frame; the $n=3$ branch is closed completely in the dossier,
the $n=5$ branch is developed to two genus-two covers over $\Q(\sqrt2)$
whose complete fake two-Selmer set has four classes, none eliminated, with
a saturated rank-two pair-field lattice and conditional
elliptic-Chabauty gates.
\item \textbf{The degree-90 toolchain}: the class of a marked half ideal
$[A_R]$ in a degree-90 $S$-class group, open, with the field itself now
computable, the splitting
$d_{90}=\dim\mathrm{Cl}_S(F_{30})[2]+\dim\ker(N)[2]$ proved, and the
short-vector route measured to exhaustion.
\end{itemize}

An exhausted-routes catalogue records every closed route with its exact
boundary, so that no successor re-runs a dead end. The program's remaining
gates are recorded with declared difficulty in the repository's task
matrix; the deepest --- deciding every one-primitive branch, closing the
zero-primitive high-support stratum by an effective coloured $S$-unit
theorem, and the final exhaustive join --- are frontier problems with no
known finite reduction, and this paper claims nothing about them.
