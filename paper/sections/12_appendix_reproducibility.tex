\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}

\subsection{Engines}

All theorem-level arithmetic in this paper is exact: integer and symbolic
computation in Python with implementation-separated replays where a result
is load-bearing, and elliptic-curve rank, torsion, and rational-point
computations in the recorded software proof modes where available. Results attributed to the official Magma Calculator V2.29-8
(the rank-zero quotients of Sections~\ref{sec:finiteness}
and~\ref{sec:frontier}, the certified-complete point lists, the descent
transcripts) enter the record exclusively as \textbf{hash-verified frozen
transcripts}: the verifier checks digests and parsed assertions and never
executes that software. One accompanying PARI/GP record corroborates only the
non-load-bearing rank-three quotient of Section~\ref{sec:finiteness}; a
second pinned record supplies the unconditional rank-zero interval for
Proposition~\ref{prop:bremnershadow}, while the verifier independently
checks its quotient map, finite-field counts, torsion and terminal parameter
elimination. No
floating-point quantity feeds any conclusion. The checked-in manuscript was
built deterministically with MiKTeX-pdfTeX 4.23 (MiKTeX 25.12), using the
three-pass \texttt{pdflatex} route at the fixed
\texttt{SOURCE\_DATE\_EPOCH}; the source also supports the Tectonic route
documented in the companion repository. It is distributed as a public
preprint companion artifact.

\subsection{The smooth-support certificates}

The complete total $3649$ in Theorem~\ref{main:smoothsupport} is imported
from von K\"anel--Matschke~\cite{VKM16}; the corresponding witnesses and
all downstream triangles, midpoint tests and $(9,7)$ arrays are rebuilt
locally. The producer and verifier do not import each other's code: one
enumerates $k+(m-k)=m$ and tests $m+k$, while the other starts with
$k+m=m+k$ and joins the other additive record. Their $D_4$
canonicalizations are also separate. These checks validate the finite
deduction, not the source theorem's original height bounds or sieve.

\subsection{Data policy}

Catalogued third-party data is cited and, where licensing is unresolved,
\textbf{never bundled}: the Fituvalu tables are referenced by locator and
digest~\cite{FIT} (URL, file name, byte count and SHA-256 in the
bibliography entry and in the repository source lock), and third-party
PDFs are cited by locator without redistribution. Web catalogue records
(Boyer's pages~\cite{BOYWEB}, Brown's note~\cite{BRO}) are used under
dated locators recorded in the repository's source locks. Bounded censuses are labelled with their frozen
bounds and are never used as evidence for an unbounded statement; where a
census executable has a recorded interface defect, only its frozen default
output is cited.

\subsection{Manifest}

The companion repository ships a manifest of SHA-256 digests covering
every shipped source byte of the public package --- scripts, certificates,
transcripts, documentation and this manuscript's sources; the compiled PDF
(a deterministic function of the pinned sources on a fixed TeX
distribution) and the regenerated results directory are deliberately
unpinned. The original verifier has a frozen expected output whose
digest is pinned inside the verifier. A separate exact verifier covers
the smooth-support catalogue and its own frozen expected receipt. The
original verifier compares its output with the frozen expectation byte for
byte. The smooth-support verifier compares exact JSON values, types and
structure, independently of whitespace and object-key order. Both require
their expected receipt and fail on its absence or on a failed comparison.
